Questions

Do I have to change how I take notes?

No. Write the way you already do. Headings and "X is Y" sentences make the best cards.

Can I bring my old notes?

Yes. Paste them in or import Markdown and Word files, and Tallbook makes cards from those too.

What happens to my notes if I stop paying?

You keep all of them. You go back to three active notebooks, and the rest become read-only.

Does it work for maths?

Yes, with LaTeX in notes and on cards.
